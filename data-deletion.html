GymTracker Data Deletion

Last updated: 4 August 2026

GymTracker (an independently developed app) has no servers of its own and no user accounts managed by us — there is no "GymTracker account" to request the deletion of, because we never store your data on our side. All training and health information stays on your phone only, and the single copy outside it is the one you choose to keep in your own personal Google Drive account.

Deleting your data is therefore something you do directly — you do not need to contact us or wait for us. The steps below cover everything the app stores.

How to delete your data

Immediate

Delete individual records

Inside the app, any workout, body measurement or cycle record can be deleted individually, directly on the screen where it appears (bin icon / "Delete" option).

Immediate

Delete everything at once

  1. Open GymTracker
  2. Go to Profile → ⚙️ Settings → Data
  3. Tap "Reset Database" and confirm

This permanently deletes every workout, measurement, plan, achievement and setting stored on the phone. It cannot be undone.

Immediate

Uninstall the app

Uninstalling GymTracker from your phone immediately removes the entire local database — Android deletes the app's data folder completely.

Connected accounts (Google Drive and Spotify)

Optional

Google Drive

The backup lives in your own Google Drive account, in a folder called GymTracker. To remove it:

  1. In the app: Settings → Google Drive → "Disconnect Google Drive" (to stop syncing)
  2. In Drive: open drive.google.com, go into the GymTracker folder and delete it — the files are yours and only you control them
  3. To revoke the app's access to your Google account entirely: myaccount.google.com/permissions
Optional

Spotify

The Spotify connection is only used to show and control music during a workout — we do not store your listening history. To disconnect:

  1. In the app: Profile → "Disconnect Spotify"
  2. To revoke access entirely: spotify.com/account/apps

What is deleted and what is kept

  • Local data (workouts, measurements, achievements, hashed PIN): deleted immediately when you reset the database or uninstall the app — with no retention period, because it never leaves your phone.
  • Google Drive backup: exists only if you created one, and stays in your account indefinitely until you delete it yourself (we have no access to delete it for you).
  • Google/Spotify session tokens: stored encrypted on the phone (Android Keystore), removed when you disconnect the account or reset/uninstall the app.
